Deep Hedging & Pricing Simulator

European options priced and risk-managed three independent ways, forced to agree: analytic Black-Scholes, autograd (AAD), and pathwise Monte Carlo (Greeks by backpropagating through simulated paths). On top of that, two models train live in the dashboard: a differential ML pricer (Savine) and a deep-hedging policy under transaction costs (Buehler).
