Deep Hedging & Pricing Simulator

European option pricing and hedging in PyTorch. Greeks are computed three independent ways (analytic Black-Scholes, autograd, pathwise Monte Carlo) and the tests check they agree. On top: a differential ML pricer (Huge & Savine, 2020) and a deep hedging policy under transaction costs (Buehler et al., 2019).
